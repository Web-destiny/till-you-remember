# Изображения

Все три исходных изображения созданы встроенным инструментом `image_gen.imagegen`, без API/CLI. Оригиналы скопированы в проект, альфа-канал спрайтов сохранён. Использование: Canvas 2D, фон 1024×1536 игровых координат, герой — сетка 4×4, персонажи — сетка 3×2. Цвет плаща героя меняется кодом только в зелёно-бирюзовой части палитры.

Промпт:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background map for a playable 2D top-down pixel art RPG, portrait 1024x1536. Primary request: a richly detailed old European medieval fantasy village and adjacent forest, in the visual style of the user's SECOND attached reference: exquisite hand placed small pixels, textured terracotta roof tiles, half timbered cream plaster houses, deep lush green trees, hanging ivy, tiny flower pots, stacked barrels, worn limestone cobblestone streets and lovely warm afternoon sunlight. Camera fixed orthographic RPG three-quarter top-down, exactly like classic high-quality 2D JRPGs, NOT isometric diamonds, NOT 3D render, no depth of field. COMPOSITION IS CRITICAL because this will be a walkable game map. No characters, no interface, no writing. An uninterrupted broad walkable stone street runs from bottom center (50% width) to top center. The center 28% of width is always unobstructed navigable ground, no houses or props there. At the bottom 65%-100% is a beautiful village plaza with houses on left and right edges, a small well at x25% y83%, open central street. In the middle 35%-65%, the central street runs through lush woodland with open grassy clearings on left and right connected to central road. A narrow stream crosses entire map horizontally at y40% with a wide stone bridge across the center, bridge width 28% of image. In the top 0%-35% are overgrown ruins, an ancient stone arch at center y28%, and an open circular shrine platform at center x50% y13% with a mystical purple portal. Dense woodland and stone ruins border the sides. Entire map completely filled with art. Some environmental cyan and violet flowers. High visual quality, dense intricate true pixel art with a restrained harmonious palette and consistent 2 pixel clusters, dramatic tree shadows, cozy nostalgic atmosphere. No people, no monsters, no UI, no logos. Make terrain a continuous complete playable world map. The portrait village from the attached references is a style reference only; create an original map with the specified layout.

## `dist/assets/elian.png`

Прозрачный фон. Промпт:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG sprite sheet for a 2D pixel art RPG. Create an exact regular 4 columns x 4 rows grid on a transparent 1024x1024 square canvas. Sixteen equal 256x256 cells. Every cell contains the SAME full body young male fantasy adventurer character, a chestnut brown haired young swordsman with a teal forest green cloak, leather belt, dark brown boots, small satchel, ivory tunic, steel shoulder armor, sword at side. Pixel art highly detailed sprite style as in the user's medieval village reference, crisp pixel clusters and rich shading, no antialiasing. Character footprint about 100 pixels wide and 170 pixels tall inside each cell, feet always at y=220 relative to cell, character centered horizontally, no overlap between cells. Top row: four frames of walking toward the viewer (front). Second row: four frames walking to the left (left profile). Third row: four frames walking to the right (right profile). Fourth row: four frames walking away from viewer (back). Each row follows contact left, passing, contact right, passing animation sequence. Consistent proportions and details across all sixteen sprites. Orthographic slightly top down classic 2D JRPG camera. Transparent background, NO ground, NO shadows, NO grid lines, NO text, NO labels, NO decorative framing, NO other objects. Production game sprite sheet, not concept art. Pixel art, not vector or 3D.
